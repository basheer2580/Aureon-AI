A GOOD PLACE TO BEGIN / 003

From first click
to first thought.

A few simple steps to set up your workspace securely.

01

Choose your platform

Use a release card above to download the build for your device. Install only files from the official Aureon distribution link, and check the version and publisher details before opening.

↘
02

Keep your device protected

If SmartScreen or macOS blocks an unsigned build, don't disable system protection or override the warning unless Aureon has published a signed, verified release and you have confirmed its signature. Until then, use the web workspace instead.

↘
03

Connect your model providers

Provider credentials are configured by your deployment administrator on the server. Create keys only in each provider's official dashboard, then ask your administrator to configure them securely. Never paste API keys into chat or share them in the browser.

↘
04

Sign in and make it yours

Use a verified email or mobile number with a strong password. Your threads stay in your sidebar, while model options reflect the providers configured for your account.

AUREON / PRIVACY FRAMEWORK

Your work stays yours.

Aureon collects account identifiers (verified email address or mobile number), session access timestamps, project code assets, and media-render usage statistics.

This information is used to operate accounts, manage credits, provide requested features, and isolate each account's workspace. Aureon does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Supabase and the model or media provider selected for a request process the data needed to deliver those features; prompts may be sent to that selected provider.

Generated media is held in private storage and served using temporary links. When you choose to publish a website, its published code is accessible at its public site path; do not publish confidential material.

Account data is retained while needed to provide the service. You can request account closure from your workspace profile menu; deletion removes the account and associated records and assets, subject to any legally required retention.
